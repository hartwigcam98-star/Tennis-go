How to play

AimSwipe up from anywhere on the screen. The angle of your swipe sets the direction. DepthThe length of your swipe sets how deep it lands. Too long sails it out. PowerThe speed of your swipe sets the pace. A quick flick hits hard, a slow drag is safe. PreviewWhile you swipe, the target on their court shows where it will land. Green is in, red is out. TimingNone. Swipe any time once the ball is coming and your player swings when it arrives. MoveTap anywhere on your side of the court to move there. Tap near the net after a good shot to come in and volley. SmashAt the net, a ball over your head becomes an overhead. Your player backs up under it and swings when you swipe. LobWhen they rush the net, a long, slow swipe floats a lob over their head. PressureMake them run and they miss, or float back a weak, short ball. When you're the one stretched, the hint says so: swipe safer to keep it in. StaminaRunning drains the bar under each name; you get some back between points. Tired players move slower and spray shots. Make your opponent run. TimingThe ball glows just before it reaches you. Swipe while it glows for a perfect hit: tighter and a little harder. ServeSwipe up into the glowing box.
